## 1. WHAT THIS APP IS
Personal sports-betting scanner (ARB / MIDDLE / +EV) across ~16 books. Polls odds on a schedule, verifies every candidate twice, sends WhatsApp alerts with exact bets, tracks confirmations, and runs a long-term "Brain" that manages per-book account safety. One global money mode: SIMULATED (default, works with fake data out of the box) or LIVE. Single user, multi-profile.

## 2. HARD RULES (locked)
1. **No prices/stakes until verification.** Pending cards: book + selection + odds only. Verified: exact stakes per leg ("│ BET $35 ↗").
2. **Line-move tolerance gate**: default 5%, user-set 0–100% in SETTINGS → RISK & BANKROLL. Edge may weaken that much (relative) at the 75s recheck and still promote; otherwise KILLED — FAILED_VERIFICATION.
3. **No skip feature. No promo strategy.** Mix = ARB / MIDDLE / EV, sliders locked to 100.
4. **One sport per book**; Pinnacle sharp-exempt (any sport, hedge leg, no heat).
5. **Kelly (0.25, cap 5%) against ONE total bankroll** ($10,000 CAD default), never per book.
6. **Data kept forever, export only.** Nightly backups ×14. (Never show the words "append-only", "ghost", "picker", "grader", "CLV", "gatekeeper" — say "closing price edge", "double verification".)
7. Soccer arbs may have **3 legs** (home/draw/away).
8. **FRESH counts down; STALE counts up** + translucent-white REFRESH? button. Verified stale trades auto-remove after X min (SETTINGS → SCAN RULES stepper, default 10).
9. **Quiet hours** 00:00–08:00: no scans, no sends.

## 3. PIPELINE
Scan (base 20 min; 5–8 min <2h to start) → candidate (PENDING, card shows "CHECKING AGAIN IN m:ss" counting down from VERIFY GAP 75s) → kill battery (ONE_SPORT_RULE, HEAT_GATE, SHARP_VELOCITY_CAP 3/day/book, MARKET_BREADTH_CAP 2/market/book/week, ROUNDING_DESTROYS_MARGIN, QUOTE_STALE) → recheck + tolerance gate → VERIFIED LIVE (stakes computed: round $5, min $10, cap 5% of total; WhatsApp sent; FRESH countdown then STALE count-up) → user acts (reply 1 SECURED / 3 LIMITED, or in-app) → CONFIRMED / UNCONFIRMED / EXPIRED → settle WON/LOST. Kills go to the Graveyard with reason strings.
